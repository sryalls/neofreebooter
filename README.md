# Neofreebooter

**A very rough bag of ideas. But it could be a thing.**

What happens when technology lets a person or a small group do work that used to require a much larger organisation — and a community helps them turn that capability into a decent life?

AI, open knowledge, software, affordable fabrication and networks may make productive independence possible at smaller scales. The working idea here is to surround that independence with shared tools, learning, mutual support and institutions that reduce the cost and risk of going it alone.

Think autonomous stallholders and smallholders, supported by a contemporary commons: people earning, making and cooperating, with somewhere to learn, equipment they can access, people they can ask, and infrastructure they can help govern.

The ambition is **a decent, dignified, somewhat prosperous life**, and wider access to the means of achieving it.

## Where this stands

This is an exploratory notebook becoming a repository. It contains a provisional model, connections to existing practice, unanswered questions and possible experiments. We have not demonstrated that the pieces form a viable whole. Even the name is provisional.

The starting point is a conversation about technological change, mutualism, commons and community organisation. A later encounter with Jessica Friday’s catalogue of alternative economic movements suggested a useful way to find existing building blocks. The documents here organise those discussions into an initial reading route; their [provenance and limits](docs/provenance.md) are explicit.

## Existing building blocks

Much of what we might need already exists. The question is how to make it accessible and connect it usefully.

- **Knowledge and designs:** MIT OpenCourseWare, FreeSewing and other open repositories supply material to learn from and build with. [Explore the commons resources](docs/resources/README.md#knowledge-and-designs).
- **Tools and practical learning:** tool libraries, workshops and repair organisations offer examples of shared equipment and human support. [Explore shared infrastructure](docs/resources/README.md#tools-and-practical-learning).
- **Ownership and durable assets:** cooperatives and community ownership offer institutions that participants can own and govern. [Explore ownership](docs/resources/README.md#ownership-and-assets).
- **Economic connections:** Community Wealth Building offers approaches to procurement, ownership and the circulation of wealth. [Explore circulation](docs/resources/README.md#wealth-circulation-and-purpose).
- **Discovery:** Friday’s list is a source of leads to examine using our own criteria. [See how we connect the list](docs/resources/friday-crosswalk.md).

A resource being listed means it may help answer a question. It does not mean its operators endorse this project, or that it proves the overall model works.

## Help develop it

Useful contributions include a better explanation, a practical example, evidence against an assumption, a learning pathway someone has actually used, or an account of something that failed.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). A good next contribution would connect one real resource to one real need, explain the prerequisites, and identify what support a beginner would still need.

The next milestone is a small, evidence-bearing parts catalogue and one modest experiment. The wider movement remains a possibility to investigate.
