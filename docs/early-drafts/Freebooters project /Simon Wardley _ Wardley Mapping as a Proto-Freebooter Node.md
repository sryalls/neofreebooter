## **Simon Wardley / Wardley Mapping as a Proto-Freebooter Node**

### **a) Commons-based mapping and doctrine**

- Wardley Maps are openly shared, discussed in community forums, and used as a collaborative tool for sense-making rather than proprietary corporate strategy.[community.wardleymaps.com](https://community.wardleymaps.com/t/cartographer-needs-in-2018/145/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[Boundaryless](https://www.boundaryless.io/podcast/simon-wardley/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- The practice is taught through distributed “cartographer” communities. One post describes Wardley mapping as a *soft skill for collaboration*, helping people frame shared assumptions and interdependencies.[community.wardleymaps.com](https://community.wardleymaps.com/t/cartographer-needs-in-2018/145/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This resembles a freebooter commons: a shared tool for situational awareness, available to anyone able to learn it, and used to stabilise coordination across loosely connected nodes.

### **b) Mapping as a coordination and intelligence commons**

- Wardley emphasises mapping *capital and value chains* across contexts (organizations, nation-states, ecosystems).[Boundaryless](https://www.boundaryless.io/podcast/simon-wardley/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynefin_framework?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- His focus on doctrine, pre-mortems/post-mortems, and evolving practice over decades shows how communities can build strategic intelligence over time—even when formal institutional support is weak or absent.[Boundaryless](https://www.boundaryless.io/podcast/simon-wardley/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[Cynefin.io](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Main_Page?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

That aligns neatly with our Neo-Freebooter strategic overlay: mapping helps federated nodes sense where tools and practices are evolving, where to build local capacity vs. where to recombine shared infrastructure or “buy stable commodities.”

### **c) Distributed community and evolutionary lag**

- Wardley notes a 20–30 year lag between new organisational practices emerging and large corporate adoption.[Boundaryless](https://www.boundaryless.io/podcast/simon-wardley/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[community.wardleymaps.com](https://community.wardleymaps.com/t/cartographer-needs-in-2018/145?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- He emphasizes that strategy needs situational awareness of the evolving landscape, rather than chasing the latest hype.[Boundaryless](https://www.boundaryless.io/podcast/simon-wardley/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[community.wardleymaps.com](https://community.wardleymaps.com/t/cartographer-needs-in-2018/145?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Freebooter nodes—small, nimble, and embedded in local realities—can exploit that lag to adopt emergent practices earlier, experiment, and feed back into later generations of institutional change.

### **Summary: Wardley as proto-freebooter**

Wardley Mapping and its community practices tick many proto-freebooter boxes:

- a shared commons tool
- distributed learning communities
- strategic intelligence emerging from mapping, iteration, doctrine, and distributed coordination
- evolutionary, practice-centric adaptation rather than corporate hype chase

## **The Cynefin Company as a Commons Institution?**

The Cynefin Company (and its parent Cognitive Edge) are more mixed. Still, there are freebooter-friendly elements in the way they operate.

### **a) Open, networked sense-making**

- Cynefin’s tools (SenseMaker®, the Cynefin framework, training programmes) are applied broadly across government, NGOs, and communities.[kmworld.com](https://www.kmworld.com/articles/news/news-analysis/the-cynefin-centre-life-after-ibm-14542.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[vg.linkedin.com](https://vg.linkedin.com/company/the-cynefin-company?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[The Open University](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/hybrid-working-planning-the-future/content-section-4.2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- The Cynefin Centre presents itself as a membership-based **action research hub**, offering participatory methods for complexity and sense-making.[kmworld.com](https://www.kmworld.com/articles/news/news-analysis/the-cynefin-centre-life-after-ibm-14542.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[The Cynefin Co](https://thecynefin.co/common-meaning/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- Snowden has explicitly positioned the Cynefin work as *training people to “do the work themselves”*, rather than creating dependency on consultants.[kmworld.com](https://www.kmworld.com/articles/news/news-analysis/the-cynefin-centre-life-after-ibm-14542.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

These practices suggest a co-created commons of sense-making tools, with a strong emphasis on empowering distributed actors rather than imposing top-down solutions—all consistent with a Neo-Freebooter ethos.

### **b) Complexity literacy as a modern literacy**

- Cynefin teaches people to distinguish between **clear/complicated/complex/chaotic/disordered domains**, and to use different decision strategies (probe-sense-respond, safe-to-fail experiments, swarming, crisis stabilisation) accordingly.[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynefin_framework?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[edgeofpossible.com](https://edgeofpossible.com/using-the-cynefin-framework-for-leading-social-impact/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- Its focus on complexity literacy, sense-making retreats, and community-driven feedback loops complements our strategic overlay: providing practical tools for distributed nodes to assess their domain and respond adaptively.

### **c) But not exactly grassroots**

- Unlike Wardley Mapping’s community-led diffusion, Cynefin is more institutional: built around consulting, training, workshops, and membership offerings. There's more of a top-down consulting‐style education model, which sometimes risks replicating provider/client dynamics.[kmworld.com](https://www.kmworld.com/articles/news/news-analysis/the-cynefin-centre-life-after-ibm-14542.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[vg.linkedin.com](https://vg.linkedin.com/company/the-cynefin-company?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- That said, the stated mission of empowering people to self-run complex-system sensing and adaptation mitigates that risk—especially if insulated from pure corporate consultancy or coercion.

## **Proposed Case Study Entry: “Wardley + Cynefin Commons”**

Here’s a sketch of how this could look as a formal case-study entry in the Neo-Freebooter compendium:

### **Wardley & Cynefin as Strategy and Sense-making Commons**

**Context**: Two overlapping communities and toolsets (Wardley Mapping and Cynefin sense-making) have evolved over the last two decades as shared infrastructure for strategy, complexity literacy, and organisational adaptation.

**Problem Addressed**: Traditional top-down strategic planning and consulting often fail in fast-changing, uncertain environments. Actors lack situational awareness, evolutionary insight into technology & practice, and distributed coordination tools.

**Approach / Practices**:

- Wardley Mapping: community-led mapping of value chains and practice evolution, doctrine tables, pre/post mortems, and distributed cartography.
- Cynefin / SenseMaker: participatory, complexity-aware sense-making tools and training, tailored to emergent behaviours and distributed adaptation.
- Shared teaching, open‐source and Creative Commons publications, workshops, and global network of skilled “cartographers” and “sense-makers.”

**Freebooter Principles in Action**:

- *Material reciprocity*: Mapping tools and sense-making methods are shared openly, contributed to, and improved through community feedback.
- *Commons engagement*: The Wardley cartographer community explicitly collaborates across geographies and institutional backgrounds; Cynefin Centre aims to train agencies to “self-serve” rather than outsource.
- *T-shaped literacy uplift*: Participants gain strategic literacy (mapping, sense-making, evolutionary dynamics) that complements their domain expertise.
- *Strategic overlay*: Mapping and sense-making provide local nodes with anticipatory intelligence (VSM System 4), doctrine alignment, and emergent adaptation capacity.
- *Lowering barriers*: Both tools are taught to non-experts, practiced in local and global workshops, and increasingly applied by small and medium organisations rather than just large corporations.

**Lessons for Neo-Freebooters**:

1. Sense-making and strategic mapping can act as distributed commons that strengthen systemic resilience.
2. Communities of practice built around these tools provide leadership alternatives to corporate consulting or technocratic planning.
3. Embedding complexity and evolutionary literacy—as taught and practiced through Cynefin/Wardley—enhances the adaptive capacity of emergent freebooter networks.
4. Even institutional actors (think Cynefin Centre) can play a supportive role if their structure is intentionally open, participatory, and oriented toward self-sufficiency rather than consultancy.
