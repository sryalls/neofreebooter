# Open questions

[Documentation entry point](../../README.md) · [Experiments](experiments.md)

| Question | Why it matters | Useful evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which work genuinely becomes viable at smaller scales? | The technological premise is sector-dependent | Costs and outcomes from actual small producers, including failures |
| Where does dependable demand come from? | Production capability alone does not pay bills | Repeat customers, pricing and contract access |
| Who pays for mentoring and coordination? | Unpaid labour can conceal the model’s real costs | Time accounts, compensation and retention |
| Can people with fewer resources enter? | Otherwise the model may reinforce a comfortable guild caste | Participation, completion and benefits across different starting conditions |
| What benefits reach non-producers? | Security and belonging should extend beyond entrepreneurship | Access to care, shared assets and community services |
| What should remain individual and what should be mutual? | Sharing creates overhead as well as savings | Total costs, asset utilisation and participant preferences |
| How resilient is the network? | Common clients and suppliers can create correlated risks | Dependence maps, reserves and recovery from disruption |
| Can governance resist capture? | Thin formal structures may hide strong informal hierarchies | Decision records, contested decisions and successful exits |
| What happens to the commons over time? | Funding and maintenance determine longevity | Update effort, contributor turnover and resource reliability |
| Do benefits survive founder departure? | A community needs continuity beyond its initiators | Succession and a documented handover |
