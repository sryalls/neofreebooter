# Draft reading guide

[Documentation entry point](../../README.md) · [Complete draft index](../early-drafts/README.md)

The Start Here documents are the current, shorter synthesis. The early drafts are working material: they include exploratory arguments, overlapping versions, case notes, outlines and ideas that have not all been checked. You do not need to read them to understand the project. Start with the current documents below, then follow a draft only when you want to explore a particular thread.

## Reading paths

- **A quick conceptual route:** proposition → T-shaped capability and accessibility → commons and community → transition pathways.
- **A practical route:** resource map → local case studies → case-study compendium → pressure tests and limits.
- **A critical route:** inclusion and material emancipation → conflict, trust and cultural drift → finance, risk and environmental limits.
- **A systems route:** architecture → commons maps → VSM and Agile → Wardley mapping and strategic overlays.

These are discovery paths, not endorsements of every claim in the notes. Some files are near-duplicates or alternate versions; the [complete draft index](../early-drafts/README.md) groups them by topic and identifies paired versions where relevant.
