# Possible first experiments

[Documentation entry point](../../README.md) · [Open questions](open-questions.md)

These are suggestions for discussion. No programme, partners or budget have been agreed.

## 1. One usable learning pathway

Choose one modest outcome, such as completing a simple sewn item or a small software tool. Connect a suitable open resource, a beginner guide, peer support and a mentor. Compare the participant’s starting capability with the finished result.

Record prerequisite gaps, time, costs, assistance, errors and whether the instructions can help another learner. Include mentor and curator time in the cost. A useful finding could be that the proposed route is too demanding and needs redesign.

## 2. One shared resource

Investigate an existing workshop or tool library before acquiring equipment. Identify one task participants want to accomplish and document access conditions, travel, supervision, consumables, scheduling and maintenance.

Compare shared access with realistic alternatives. Sharing succeeds if the full arrangement improves access or reduces costs enough to justify coordination.

## 3. One connection to paid demand

Find a real small customer need and determine whether a participant or small team can meet it using the pathway and infrastructure. Record the full cost, quality requirements, payment and repeat demand.

Evaluate whether earnings remain worthwhile after support and overheads. An isolated sale shows less than a repeatable livelihood.

## A minimal experiment record

State the question, participants’ consent, starting conditions, scope, costs, outcome and what would cause the experiment to stop or change. Keep private participant details out of the repository. Publish a short account of the result, including difficulties and unsuccessful approaches.
