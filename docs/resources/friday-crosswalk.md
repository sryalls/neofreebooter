# Connecting Friday’s list to the model

[Resource map](README.md) · [Architecture](../architecture.md)

**Scope:** selected categories from [Jessica Friday’s catalogue](https://connectioning.substack.com/p/the-300-movements-to-a-fair-economy), checked 7 October 2026. This is our functional classification. It does not reproduce her catalogue or adopt its rankings.

## Keep the categories distinct

An ownership arrangement, a funding mechanism and a framework for judging economic success solve different problems. Related labels may describe variants of one mechanism, but should only be collapsed after checking what differs in practice.

The working process is: identify a need, find candidate mechanisms, compare direct sources, examine implementation evidence, then test an appropriate connection locally.

## What the list does not settle

It does not establish customer demand, pay for coordination, resolve care obligations, specify governance interfaces or demonstrate that autonomous production is viable in a particular sector.

It helps broaden the search. The evidence-bearing parts catalogue remains work to do; see [open questions](../research/open-questions.md).
