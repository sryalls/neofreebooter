# Resource map

[Documentation entry point](../../README.md) · [Friday crosswalk](friday-crosswalk.md) · [Evaluation template](../../templates/resource-assessment.md)

This is a starter map organised by the job a resource might do. The connections to Neofreebooter are our interpretations. We have not evaluated the combined model or audited these organisations’ outcomes.

**Source check: 7 October 2026.** “Checked” means the provider’s page was retrieved or surfaced in search and supports the brief description. “Lead” means retained from the discussion for later checking. Neither status is an effectiveness rating.

## Knowledge and designs

| Resource | What it supplies | Proposed connection | Status / remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/get-started/) | Openly accessible course materials | Raw commons for curated learning pathways | Checked; select appropriate courses and review reuse terms |
| [FreeSewing](https://freesewing.eu/) | Open-source software generating bespoke sewing patterns | Designs connected to mentoring, equipment and practical making | Checked; test a complete beginner pathway |
| [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/) | Candidate source of fabrication designs | A possible input to shared fabrication | Lead; page retrieval failed, individual designs and licences require review |
| [GitHub](https://github.com/) | Candidate discovery route for software projects | Code and documentation for producers and shared services | Lead; curate specific maintained repositories rather than treating the platform as a commons licence |

See [raw and overlay commons](../commons.md). None of these links supplies an entire education or guarantees a commercially usable asset.

## Ownership and assets

| Resource | What it supplies | Proposed connection | Status / remaining work |

| Credit unions, community lenders and mutual risk provision | Candidate mechanisms discussed in the model | Finance and resilience for members | Research gap; no specific provider assessed yet |

A cooperative is an ownership and governance arrangement, not a guarantee of viability. Housing, finance and risk pooling need much more specific research than this map currently contains.

## Discovery and connections

[Jessica Friday’s 300+ list](https://connectioning.substack.com/p/the-300-movements-to-a-fair-economy) is a broad discovery source. It combines movements, frameworks and approaches; the page describes its curation and evaluation as unfinished. We use it to generate leads, then follow those leads to direct sources.

The [crosswalk](friday-crosswalk.md) connects selected categories to our architecture. It is a first pass, not an exhaustive decomposition of the list.

## How to extend the map

Use the [assessment template](../../templates/resource-assessment.md). Record the problem solved, mechanism, prerequisites, cost, permissions, evidence, limitations and connection to another component. Include failures and counterexamples. Replace broad labels with concrete organisations, guides or evaluated implementations as research progresses.
