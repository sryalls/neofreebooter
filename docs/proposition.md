# The working proposition

[Documentation entry point](../README.md) · [Architecture](architecture.md) · [Open questions](research/open-questions.md)

## Productive agency at smaller scales

Our hypothesis is that some forms of sophisticated work are becoming practical for individuals and small groups through a combination of skill, AI, open knowledge, fabrication and networks. This varies considerably by sector. Lower software costs do not remove physical inputs, distribution, accreditation or the need to find customers.

A **neofreebooter** is our provisional name for someone using that capacity to exercise greater control over their livelihood. They might be a programmer, grower, repairer, designer, maker or a member of a small productive team. Conventional employment and outside customers can remain part of their life.

The project asks whether a community can make this agency more accessible, sustainable and socially useful. The desired outcome is material security and dignity, alongside autonomy, belonging and meaningful participation.

## Independence needs support

An individual can remain autonomous while sharing machinery, premises, learning, referrals and administrative work. Some needs may be better served by durable mutual institutions: housing, finance, energy, care and shared assets.

The proposed community therefore has two complementary tasks:

1. Help people acquire and exercise productive capability.
2. Reduce the costs and risks that would otherwise make independence precarious.

Income from external markets can support this ecology. Members need not source everything internally, and participation should remain worthwhile without requiring agreement on a comprehensive political programme.

## Intellectual neighbourhood

The discussion drew connections to mutualism, smallholding, guilds, commons, cooperative ownership and Stafford Beer’s Viable System Model. These are influences and questions to pursue, rather than a claim that one historical tradition already contains our solution.

Community Wealth Building adds a practical lens on ownership, procurement and economic circulation. Its established practices may supply parts of the model; our emphasis on technology-enabled individual agency is a separate hypothesis.

## The inclusion test

A comfortable network of already skilled people would be easy to imagine. A community that expands agency for people with less money, time, confidence or formal education is the harder and more important proposition.

Support must include usable routes into learning, appropriate compensation for care and mentoring, accessible participation, and benefits for people who cannot or do not want to become independent producers. Basic dignity cannot depend on entrepreneurial success.

## What would count as progress?

Evidence that members can do useful things they could not previously do; lower total costs after counting coordination and maintenance; better income security; fair access; and a community that survives the departure of its founders.

Those are outcomes to measure. The attractiveness of the architecture is not evidence that they will occur.
